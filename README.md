## Story:
Your universe was destroyed by an exo oblivion created by aliens outside of space and time. 
On this adventure, you seek shards that are pieces to making for what's called "The Crystal Of Voice" to bring back universal peace and put them together.
Boyfriend, Pico, Girlfriend and some familiar faces like Camellia from distinct different worlds come to help bring back the original timeline. But they're scattered across different worlds, and they're very different. 

Prepare a crazy, astronomical beep boop bap adventure of chaos, and torture that also includes beautiful vfx and lyrics!
Brand new mechanics that have never been seen before show up in this mod.

The SkyDecay Project is a Mania-focused fnf mod with high quality Mania charting from Osu! Mania and Quaver converted to FNF. By FNF charters and known Mania mappers to help collaborate on this mod. It's to also allow new rhythm game players to experience what Mania really is, and not something to be afraid of. This is a very experimental fnf mod, not everything will be perfect. We do everything to make sure this mod is fun and fitting to the engine. The story is chaotic with original songs included with known Mania songs. We try to be different with vocal covers so it isn't the base/original chromatics that sound off. We hope you find this experience amazing, and not a typical mod with a simple story. 

We're glad to collaborate with known mods, and we're glad to help make them better!

If you're new here in this official repo, please be careful with using the engine/mod as the engine is still work in progress, and not fully ready as a finished engine. If you want to use a base engine please consider the sdy-engine branch as the stable version till a update comes out officially. In the meantime as well, this is the sdy-project mod. So compile if you have enough space for my wip mod. Eventually, the mod will become a separate repo and privated for collaboration reasons and so not much gets leaked. So please build in your own discretion.
